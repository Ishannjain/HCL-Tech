# Assumptions and policy gaps

The original files are user-provided scraped Croma snapshots. Three official URLs were verified on 2026-10-10. Published effective dates and versions were not available in the supplied files. The source register uses an explicitly labeled snapshot version and observation date; it does not backdate policy applicability.

Dataset categories map to the matching policy table labels: LAPTOP to Laptop, MOBILE to Mobile Phones, AUDIO to Bluetooth Speaker, TELEVISION to Television (LED), and APPLIANCE to Refrigerators. This is a bounded demo mapping, not a claim that every appliance or audio device shares these conditions. New judge categories need appropriate ingested rules. The registry does not extend a laptop window to arbitrary categories.

Synthetic fees, approval limits, warranty-date conventions, and the non-returnable AUDIO category come from DEMO-OPS. DEMO-CIRCULAR changes AUDIO's window to demonstrate supersession, and DEMO-FAQ supplies a conflicting lower-authority value. These fixtures are marked synthetic in citations, metadata, UI, and source files.

Net item prices and quantities are authoritative. Original order totals are not migrated. Return reasons and statuses outside Annex C are mapped and disclosed in the conversion record. Customer tier, joined year, and payment method were absent, so the migration assigns synthetic values; they are not recovered facts.

Optional synthetic order facts represent completed quality checks, brand certification, activation state, and inventory availability. Judge records without those facts require clarification. Real Croma refunds depend on quality checks and, in some cases, replacement inventory. The tool never equates a seven-day window with unconditional cash eligibility.

Where the required schema lacks timestamps, a 48-hour deadline is referred for support verification. Refunds are estimates; no payment service is connected. What-if scenarios do not mutate accounts or returns. Previously rejected returns can be reassessed; active or completed returns prevent a duplicate request.
